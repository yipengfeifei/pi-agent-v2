# Action Profile: Email Communication

This skill prepares or sends a bounded email response.

- Check recipient, subject, required information points, and attachments separately.
- Do not turn unknown facts into assertions.
- Preserve a clear send result or a concrete reason the message was not sent.
- Subjective writing quality is secondary to the checklist and delivery state.
